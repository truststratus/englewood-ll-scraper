Opening Day 2026


  





Saturday April 25th, 2026




  • The ELL Opening day Parade is Saturday, April 26th.

  • The parade will leave the Englewood Service Center on Union Road at 9AM.

  • All teams will be in the parade.

  • All teams will need to ride in a Truck, trailer etc. No one can walk.

  • NO CANDY will be thrown. This is dictated by the City of Englewood. 

  • Find a place to gather your team (NOT THE SERVICE CENTER) together, then bring them as a team to the Service Center by 8:45AM. (it will take a couple of minutes to get everyone situated in the proper order).

  • We will be leaving from the Service Center (Union Rd., and I-70) at 9 AM.

  • The Parade will go from The Service center to Centennial Park.

  • All players will be unloaded from their parade vehicles and go onto Centennial field C3 for our Opening Ceremony.

  • After the Ceremony all players will be released to their parents and or coaches for the day.
