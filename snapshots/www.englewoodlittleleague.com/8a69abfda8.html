VOLUNTEER INFORMATION AND APPROVED LIST

New Volunteer Approval Process for Englewood Little League

To volunteer as a coach or manager for the upcoming season, please follow these steps:

  1. Player Registration: Ensure your player is registered first.
  2. Contact Your League VP: After registration, reach out to your League VP to express your interest in volunteering as a coach or manager, unless they have already contacted you.
  3. Board Approval: Once your interest is noted, your League VP will initiate the board approval process and contact you with the next steps.

 

Before engaging with players, all volunteers must complete the following requirements:

  • Board Approval
  • Background Check (required annually)
  • Abuse Awareness Training (required annually)

 

Below is the list of individuals who have successfully completed all required steps and are approved to volunteer for the 2026 season. If you are not on this list, you are not authorized to volunteer or coach in any capacity.
