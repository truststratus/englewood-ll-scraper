Note: See fundraising information below.

Multi-player discounts are available only when registering more than one child within the same sport (baseball or softball).
(Example: 2 players in softball = discount. 1 in baseball + 1 in softball = no discount. This is a Sports Connect system limitation.)

FUNDRAISING PROGRAM

Each year, ELL organizes a fundraiser to help offset league operating costs. Every player is required to participate or choose the Buy-Out option.

Participation Requirements:

  • Players sell a specified number of items.

  • Players may sell extra units to qualify for higher prize levels.

Buy-Out Option:

If you prefer not to participate in fundraising, an additional payment is required:

  • 1 child: $50

  • 2 children: $100

  • 3 children: $150

  • 4 or more children: $200

Buy-Out payment is due during the fundraising period, or it may be added at the time of registration.
