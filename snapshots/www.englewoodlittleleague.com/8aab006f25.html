Boys Minors (8-11)

  • Our Minors Division is an instructional division to prepare players for the next level of "Majors Division" and keep teaching the fundamentals of the game.
  •  Teams are assembled by drafting after tryout/evaluation sessions.  
  •  All players need to attend the tryouts (Evaluations) Times and Dates:      Schedule will be posted online
  •  Any player not attending 2/3 of the sessions will be put in a blind draw.
  • We will draft (# based on registration) teams of 12 players.
  •               All 9, 10 and 11 year olds will be drafted and put on a team.
  •          Any 8-year-olds are eligible to tryout and be drafted on to one of the 6 teams.
  •          Roster spots for 8’s will be based on registration #’s.
  •                      72 players - Non drafted 10- and 11-year-olds = 8-year-old roster spots.
  •                              **Any 8-year-old can elect not to tryout and stay in the Coach Pitch Division (7/8 Year olds).
  • Games played at Centennial and Oberer Park
  • Season starts April 25, 2026, through June 27th (1-2 games during the week, and one on Saturday)
  • Year End Tournament.
  •  Your age is based on the age chart listed below.
